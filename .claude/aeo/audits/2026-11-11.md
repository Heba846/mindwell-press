(PR #6, which covered the seven Documentation questions) looked at. Google was
checked in Heba's signed-in Chrome; this time AI Overviews appeared for all six

Answer shape only is recorded below. No sentences from any cited page are copied.

## How do I orient a new member to an ongoing group?

- **Status:** covered — `/guides/orient-new-member-therapy-group/`
- **Google:** AI Overview present. Cited, in order of appearance: getzestlife.com,
  Brighton and Hove Psychotherapy, ICANotes, a YouTube video (Nate Page), Family
  Therapy Basics, UF Counseling and Wellness Center, wearespin.com.
  thoughtkindpress.com not cited.
- **Shape:** four numbered phases — individual pre-group orientation, preparing the
  existing group, facilitating the first session, post-session follow-up. Bullet
  lists inside each, bold lead-in labels, no table, no numbers. Ends by asking the
  reader which kind of group they run. Under organic: SPIN, an r/therapists thread,
  University of Utah and UF counseling-centre pages.
- **ChatGPT:** answered with a six-area table for the pre-group meeting, a sample
  facilitator script, a timed 60-minute first-session plan, and a follow-up
  question list. Cited aristarecovery.com and referred to AGPA practice
  guidelines. thoughtkindpress.com not cited.
- **Gap vs our page:** both answers split the job into before, during and after, and
  both tell the existing group in advance. Neither is specific to rolling
  admission with daily entry. A short before/during/after summary at the top of
  our page, and a table of what to cover in the pre-group meeting, would match the
  expected shape.

## What is the difference between PHP and IOP groups?

- **Google:** AI Overview present. Cited: Charlie Health, Charta Health, The
  Psychiatric Institute of Washington, Mind Above Matter, Guidelight Health,
  Lakeside Behavioral Health, Assure Recovery, Cedar Springs Hospital.
  thoughtkindpress.com not cited.
- **Shape:** definition sentence, then one block per level of care (hours, level of
  care, goal, daily life), then a five-row comparison table (weekly hours,
  frequency, medical supervision, work or school, common path). Gives weekly-hour
  ranges for both. Closes by asking whether the need is mental health or substance
  use, adult or teen.
- **Not checked:** ChatGPT.
- **Gap vs our page:** the answer is patient-facing (who it suits). Ours is
  facilitator-facing (what changes in the group room). That difference is real and
  worth stating in the opening paragraph, since every cited source is a provider
  marketing page.
