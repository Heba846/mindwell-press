Group Openers and Closers for Adult IOP and PHP

In a rolling-admission group, a member on day one sits beside a member on day thirty, and someone too foggy to hold a pen sits across from someone answering on autopilot. This book gives you 120 openers and closers built for that room, each one ready to read aloud two minutes before group.

What facilitators say

Who it is for

Group facilitators in adult intensive outpatient (IOP) and partial hospitalization (PHP) programs, clinical trainees and interns who lead or co-lead groups, and supervisors who want a shared way to open and close sessions. It works alongside any curriculum and has a session pairing chart for Volume 1 of our Open-Enrollment Program Series.

What it is not

It is a set of read-aloud openers and closers, not a treatment curriculum or clinical training. It does not replace supervision, individual assessment or your program's procedures. Read our editorial standards.
