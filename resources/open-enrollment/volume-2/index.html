Printing and use

Print on US Letter at actual size. For ordinary worksheets, forms and facilitator cards on A4, use a layout that preserves readable text and response space. Print a test page before making a set. The folded contact card is optional and must not be reduced; use the full-page contacts sheet for larger type and writing space. Page 98 is the intentional reverse-page explanation; print the contact card single-sided.

Keep blank print masters separate from completed pages. Store completed clinical information only in approved program systems.

Permissions

Program-use permission. The purchasing professional or program may make internal copies of the designated practice pages, session plans, cards and blank implementation forms for its own staff and clients directly served. Staff may use these copies within that program; a separate purchase for each staff member is not required. This permission does not allow resale, public uploading, external distribution of blank files, or distribution of the complete digital books. Other uses require written permission from Thoughtkind Press. Keep completed clinical information in approved systems.
