About Mindwell Press

Mindwell Press is the publishing imprint of Heba El Garhy, a behavioral health author and researcher with a background in biotechnology, clinical sciences and applied psychology. She is pursuing postgraduate studies in Psychology at Cairo University and holds professional diplomas in educational psychology, counseling and human development.

Clinical titles are reviewed by practicing clinical psychologists before publication. Every book aims to do one thing well: give the reader something they can use the same day.

Mindwell Press books are educational resources. They are not a diagnostic tool or a substitute for professional treatment. If you are in crisis in the US, call or text 988.
