About Thoughtkind Press

Thoughtkind Press is the publishing imprint of Heba El Garhy, a behavioral health author and researcher with a background in biotechnology, clinical sciences and applied psychology. She is pursuing postgraduate studies in Psychology at Cairo University and holds professional diplomas in educational psychology, counseling and human development.

Clinical titles are reviewed by practicing clinicians before publication. Every book aims to do one thing well: give the reader something they can use the same day. Read about the author and our editorial standards.

Contact

For program and team licenses, review copies, or questions about a book, email hello@thoughtkindpress.com.
