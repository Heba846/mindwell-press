Privacy policy

Last updated October 19, 2026

Thoughtkind Press (thoughtkindpress.com) is the publishing imprint of Heba El Garhy. This page explains what happens to information when you visit this site.

Analytics

We use Google Analytics to understand how visitors find and use the site: for example, which pages are read, roughly which country visits come from, and whether people arrived from a search engine, a link or an ad. Google Analytics uses cookies and collects data such as your browser type, device and approximate location. We do not use it to identify you personally, and we do not combine it with other information about you. You can opt out with Google's opt-out browser add-on or by blocking cookies in your browser. See how Google uses data from sites that use its services.

Downloads and purchases

Free files on the free resources page can be downloaded without giving any information. The Rolling-Admission Group Starter Pack is delivered through Gumroad, which collects your email address to send the file; Gumroad's own privacy policy applies, and we can see the email addresses of people who downloaded it. We may email you about related resources, and every email lets you unsubscribe. Books are sold through Amazon, which handles payment and delivery under its own policies.

Email you send us

If you email hello@thoughtkindpress.com, we use your message only to reply and keep it in our mailbox.

What we don't do

We do not sell or rent personal information, and this site has no accounts, comments or forms that store personal data on our servers. Please do not send us client or patient information.

Your choices and contact

To ask what we hold about you, or to be removed from our email list, email hello@thoughtkindpress.com.

Thoughtkind Press books are educational and program-implementation resources. They do not replace clinical training, supervision, clinical judgment or your program's policies, and they are not a diagnostic tool or a substitute for treatment. If you are in crisis in the US, call or text 988.
