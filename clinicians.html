Built around the problem closed-cohort curricula skip

Most group curricula assume everyone starts and finishes together. In a rolling-admission program, the facilitator runs a group where some members are new and some have heard every skill before. These are the parts of each session written for that.

Newcomer Bridge
Every rotation session opens with a short scripted orientation that brings a first-day member up to speed without making returning members sit through a recap.
Two sets of processing questions
Each session closes with separate questions for first-time and returning members, so both get something to work on.
No session depends on another
Run the rotation in any order. A member who missed a module can catch up with one-page summaries before stepping down.
Documentation language
Sample GIRP and DAP notes for every Skills, Psychoeducation and PHP session, and a documentation cue for every Process group.
Ready-made rotation calendars
Schedules for 3-day IOP, 4-day extended IOP and 5-day PHP programs, plus a holiday and closure protocol.

About the author

Heba El Garhy is a behavioral health author and researcher with a background spanning biotechnology, clinical sciences and applied psychology. She is pursuing postgraduate studies in Psychology at Cairo University and holds professional diplomas in educational psychology, counseling and human development. Her work focuses on turning clinical frameworks into tools that hold up in a real session.

These books are educational and program-implementation resources for licensed and supervised professionals. They do not replace clinical judgment, supervision or your program's policies, and they are not a diagnostic tool or a substitute for treatment.
